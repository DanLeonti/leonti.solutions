① The CompanyWho we are

Engineer's depth. Consultant's clarity. We sit between technical systems and the people who need answers from them.

Leonti Solutions is a technology consultancy built for the gap most teams struggle with: the space between what a system can do and what the business actually needs from it. We design solutions, build the integrations, and translate what the data says into what to do next.

Our work spans blockchain analytics and compliance, venture capital operations, and defense-grade program delivery — and we ship in React, TypeScript, Python and Swift. We stay close to the code, because staying close to the code keeps our judgment sharp.
